This policy explains what personal data is handled when you visit this website or email us, and what rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Visiting this website

This website is hosted by Netlify, Inc., 101 2nd Street, San Francisco, CA 94105, USA, as our service provider. To deliver the pages to you, its servers process technical data such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the date and time of your visit. This is necessary to run the website and keep it secure, which is our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The data is kept only for as long as needed for those purposes.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. We do not use your data for profiling or automated decisions.

Emailing us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use this to reply and to handle your request. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) or, if your message is about working with us, steps towards a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Our email runs on Google Workspace, provided by Google. If your message concerns an artist or project, we may share it with the people involved in order to handle it. We keep messages for as long as needed to deal with the matter, and longer where the law requires it, for example for tax and accounting records.

Emailing us is your choice. Without your message and address, we simply can't reply.

Links to other websites

Our pages link to services such as Instagram, Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube. Nothing is sent to them unless you click a link. Once you do, their own privacy policies apply.

Transfers outside the EU

Netlify and Google may process data outside the EU, including in the United States. Such transfers rely on safeguards recognised by the GDPR, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

We do not sell your data.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to your personal data, and ask us to correct or delete it, restrict its use, or give you a copy of it. You can also object to processing based on our legitimate interests. To do any of this, email axr@artsxrecs.com.
